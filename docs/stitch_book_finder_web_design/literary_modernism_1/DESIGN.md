## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes classical bibliophilia with modernist architectural rigor. Designed for contemplative digital reading sanctuaries, rare edition curation, and intellectual discourse, the visual tone balances archival gravitas with contemporary digital precision.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Refined Literary Glassmorphism**:
- Atmosphere: Subterranean midnight chambers, archival library vaults, and focused reading environments.
- Emotional Tone: Quiet reverence, scholarly dignity, intellectual focus, and bespoke craftsmanship.
- Stylistic Treatment: High-contrast serif headlines paired with ultra-legible geometric grotesques. Surfaces employ atmospheric translucent layers, delicate border illumination, and warm gilded focal points amidst deep nocturnal slate voids.

## Colors

The palette establishes an interplay between nocturnal ambient depths and luminous focal accents. 

### Foundation Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`)**: Deep midnight slate. Absorbs distraction; serves as the absolute bottom canvas.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0F172A`)**: Charcoal background for primary canvas framing and broad visual partitions.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#162032`)**: Recessed card surfaces and quiet utility backgrounds.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#1E293B`)**: Elevated reading cards, modals, and interactive hover planes.

### Accents & Identity
- **Primary / Illumination (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm archival amber-gold. Used for primary calls-to-action, key badges, rating states, and critical literary accents.
- **Primary Deep (`#D97706`)**: Grounded antique gold for pressed button states, active tabs, and refined framing.
- **Secondary / Kinetic (`#3B82F6`)**: Electric indigo. Reserved for structural links, dynamic reading progress, and verified scholar tags.
- **Secondary Light (`#60A5FA`)**: Highlighting annotations, code/lexicon links, and active text selection states.

### Contrast & Typography
- **Typography Primary (`#F8FAFC`)**: Crisp off-white. Dedicated to editorial display heads and primary reading body text for maximum clarity.
- **Typography Secondary (`#E2E8F0`)**: Soft stone parchment for metadata, subheadings, and interactive label states.
- **Typography Muted (`#94A3B8`)**: Subdued slate for structural subtext, book spines, timestamps, and secondary captions.
- **Structural Outlines (`#334155`)**: Slate border stroke used with variable alpha channels (30% to 60%) for crisp delineation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between the classical, high-contrast character of **Playfair Display** and the engineered neutrality of **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter**.

- **Playfair Display**: Strictly applied to editorial landmarks, book titles, chapter openings, and major showcase callouts. It imbues literary tradition without compromising sharpness on high-density displays.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Carries all reading passages and continuous descriptive blocks. Its wide apertures and balanced proportions maintain effortless legibility against dark slate backgrounds.
- **Inter**: Governs precision micro-copy, navigation nodes, tabular figures, timestamps, and compact interface metrics.
- Long-form reading containers must enforce a strict `max-width: 68ch` to emulate classical book margins and preserve optical tracking comfort.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an anchored 12-column fluid grid system, bound by generous structural margins to create an editorial cadence reminiscent of rare manuscript folios.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column layout, `margin-lg` (4rem) outer buffer, maximum content container capped at `1440px`. Column gutters hold at `gutter-lg` (2.5rem).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `margin` (1.5rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Side panels collapse into fluid overlays.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Dense components stack vertically with horizontal scrolling ribbons for metadata tags and shelf carousels.

### Spacing Rhythm
Interface density balances generous external breathing room with compact internal component grouping:
- Micro spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) handles badge insets, metadata pairs, and icon-to-label bindings.
- Standard spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) dictates interior card padding and vertical stack flow.
- Macro spacing (`space-xl`, `space-2xl`) establishes separation between thematic book collections and primary editorial showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through translucent glass substrates, selective light-bleed backdrops, and fine structural boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Stratification
- **Recessed / Base Floor**: `#0B0F17` canvas with a fine dot-grid or subtle noise matrix (2% opacity) to evoke rich bookbinding stock.
- **Level 1 (Archive Decks)**: `#0F172A` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined by a 1px solid border of `#334155` at 40% opacity.
- **Level 2 (Active Artifacts / Cards)**: `#1E293B` at 70% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is `#334155` at 60% opacity. Subtle top highlight using a gradient border fading from `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)` to `transparent`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays / Reading Modals)**: `#162032` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Encased in a perimeter shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 0 1px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`.

### Glow Borders & Illumination
- Interactive cards feature an ambient illumination on `:hover`: the 1px perimeter border transitions to `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.45)`, accompanied by a diffuse outer bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -5px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
- Electric Indigo focuses are reserved for systemic reading tools (scrubbers, highlights, terminal notes) using `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances architectural structure with comfortable ergonomics, utilizing **Soft (Level 1)** rounding throughout.

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: Fixed at `0.375rem` to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`). Sharp enough to recall cut book paper and crisp bookplates, yet softened for digital viewports.
- **Inputs & Controls**: `0.25rem` (`rounded`) to preserve architectural solidity.
- **Pills / Metadata Chips**: Retain an exception-level full pill radius (`9999px`) to create optical variety against rectilinear book covers and text columns.
- **Book Cover Mockups**: Strict `0.125rem` radius on outer edges, with an inner left-side simulated spine spine crease via an inset shadow gradient.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Gilded Lit)**: Filled with `#F59E0B`, text in `#0B0F17` (Inter Bold). Flat finish with a subtle inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`). Hover triggers subtle brightening to `#FBBF24` and a soft gold perimeter glow.
- **Secondary (Archival Glass)**: Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)` with 1px border `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.8)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.95)` with border color ascending to `#60A5FA`.
- **Ghost / Text**: Transparent background, text `#E2E8F0`. Hover transitions to `#F59E0B` with an underline offset of 4px.

### Cards & Bookplates
- Constructed with multi-tier glassmorphism: background `rgba(22, 32, 50, 0.7)`, backdrop blur `16px`, bordered by 1px `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`.
- Headers within cards use `Playfair Display` in `#F8FAFC`. 
- Book covers display within elevated slots, featuring a simulated binding spine shadow and ambient dark drop shadow (`0 8px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`).

### Input Fields
- Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`, border 1px `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.8)`. Typography is `Inter` 14px in `#F8FAFC`.
- Focus state transforms the border to `#F59E0B` with an auxiliary ring of `0 0 0 2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`. Placeholder text sits comfortably in `#94A3B8`.

### Chips & Tags
- **Category Chips**: Small, pill-shaped tags (`label-md`). Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`, border 1px `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.4)`, text `#E2E8F0`.
- **Rare Edition / Curator Badges**: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, border 1px `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`, text `#F59E0B` (`label-caps`).

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Items separated by hairline dividers: `1px solid rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.3)`.
- Primary list text set in `#F8FAFC`, paired with trailing timestamps or metadata in `#94A3B8` (`label-md`). Hovering over list rows generates a soft gradient sweep: `linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.4) 0%, transparent 100%)`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkboxes: Square frames (4px corner radius) with border `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.9)` on dark fill. Checked state renders a solid `#F59E0B` fill with `#0B0F17` iconography.
- Switch tracks: Muted slate track `#1E293B` activating to `#3B82F6` with a smooth sliding porcelain thumb (`#F8FAFC`).

### Reading Progress Indicator
- Slim 2px tracking rail spanning article/chapter tops. Rail base `#1E293B`; active indicator filled with a gradient transitioning from `#F59E0B` to `#60A5FA`.
